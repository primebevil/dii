# ADR-0015: DII Is Access Distribution, Not Capability Aggregation

Status: Accepted, 2026-07-27, from the Week-4 variety experiment review. Builds on ADR-0002, ADR-0004, ADR-0005, ADR-0006, and ADR-0009. Rests on the results in docs/Variety_Experiment_Findings.md and revises the emphasis of docs/Pod_Aggregation_Red_Team.md and the Architecture Overview.

## Context

Week 4 ran the variety experiment, the one open empirical question the pod-aggregation red team named: on the work DII's target users do, does a pool of diverse floor-class models beat the single best model in that pool. The full run came back inconclusive-to-null on this dated roster. Two of the four measurement instruments were not trustworthy (the judge tied every open-ended answer, reasoning was saturated at the models' ceiling), the one clean signal was a narrow long-context benefit that reads as specialization rather than diversity (the oracle ceiling equals both the router and the ensemble, so picking the right single model already captures it), and the one robust secondary finding was that spending equal compute on a single model does not pay and can even hurt. The experiment did its job: it killed a pilot fluke before it became a pitch. But it did not, and on reflection could not, establish variety as a reason the network exists.

The deeper realization sits above the number. Variety is a capability argument, a claim that the pool is better than a single model. The red team found, repeatedly, that DII loses whenever it argues capability and holds whenever it argues access and independence. For the served consumer, the person with no or limited access, a single capable floor model, available under conditions they can accept, is the entire value; whether a pool would beat that model by a few points is immaterial to someone who had nothing a moment earlier. Diversity was only ever load-bearing for one user in one future, the member who already runs a good local model and asks why they should pool at all, and ADR-0005 already answered why members participate with mission and community rather than personal benefit. Diversity was therefore never the reason the network exists on either side of it. This record makes that explicit and realigns the architecture to match.

## Decision

DII's thesis is access distribution: putting a capable, floor-level model (ADR-0006) into the hands of people who lack access, under conditions they can accept, meaning no counterparty who can switch them off, no content gate beyond the hard legal lines, and no requirement to send their data outside a boundary they trust. This is the load-bearing reason the network exists, standing with the mission motivation (ADR-0005) and served by the reliable floor. Model diversity, aggregation for variety, is demoted from thesis to feature. A pod that happens to reach many models offers welcome range, but that range is never the reason to exist and outward messaging must not lead with it, consistent with the red team's scorecard.

The unit of value is a node willing to serve a floor-capable model to someone who needs it. A node serving a single model within its capability is a full participant; multiple diverse models per pod is not required for the network to deliver its value. Consequently, node-to-node aggregation, the overflow path where one node borrows a model a peer holds and it does not, is reclassified from core mechanism to optional optimization. The load-bearing primitive is the consumer ingress established in ADR-0004: a consumer with no node of their own reaching a willing node. The Week-3 overflow transport (ADR-0011) remains validated and available for pods that want it, but the network's guarantee no longer depends on it.

What the coordination layer still owes is availability, not variety. Even with one model per node, a consumer needs to reach a node that is actually up and not saturated, so the decentralized directory (ADR-0009) keeps its job, but that job narrows from "find diverse or better capability" to "find an available node serving the floor." Redundancy of access replaces diversity of models as the thing the network coordinates.

"Who can use them" becomes the central open problem. The hard part of access distribution is consumer admission and abuse resistance, serving capable models to strangers for free without drowning in Sybil abuse or the real, rivalrous cost of serving, and none of that is a model-quality problem. The identity work seeded by docs/Identity_Note_From_Prototype.md and docs/Governance_And_Abuse_Resistance.md is therefore promoted from a Week-4 loose end to the next load-bearing decision. Wherever the word "free" appears in messaging it carries the qualifier "under conditions others will not or cannot be trusted to offer," so the proposition stays distinct from the free hosted tiers that already exist and win on convenience.

## Consequences

The architecture simplifies and should be re-described to match. Capability manifests and cross-node capability routing become optimizations rather than requirements, the consumer-ingress path is the spine, and the Architecture Overview should lead with access distribution and mark aggregation optional. The Week-3 prototype is not devalued by this: the part that survives as the core, the consumer ingress, is exactly the part ADR-0004 made first-class, and the overflow work stands as a proven optional capability.

The variety experiment is repositioned as settled enough to act on. It killed a false positive, showed that compute alone does not pay, and surfaced a narrow specialization signal on long-context; that is a complete enough result. No further effort goes to repairing its broken instruments for a cleaner verdict unless a specific messaging need for the number arises. The eval/ rig is retained, not as a thesis prover but as a "good enough capability" measuring tool, including for sizing which real workloads a floor node can serve.

Messaging drops any capability or diversity lead and stands on access-under-conditions, mission, and sovereignty, the legs the red team found durable. The red team's open question on whether variety is real and measurable is answered enough for decisions: treat variety as a feature, and treat any further empirical refinement of it as optional. The red team's remaining threads, where size aggregation sits and how much the access leg depends on the Sybil problem staying tractable, fold into the promoted identity work. Any earlier claim, in the docs or the messaging, that presented aggregation as the reason the network exists is downgraded to a feature by this record.

## Open questions

The identity and consumer-admission design is now the next decision: how a willing node admits and fair-use-limits unaffiliated consumers with no central authority and no behavioral-reputation store, per the governance notes and ADR-0013. Whether availability-redundancy needs any active coordination beyond a signed, gossiped directory, or whether independent nodes plus that directory suffice, is open. And whether to formally retire the node-to-node overflow transport in the reference node or keep it as an available option is deferred; it is kept available for now, and not required.
